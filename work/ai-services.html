AI services market strategy

I’m leading a project team assessing potential AI services for BYU Marriott’s Marketing Lab. We’re looking at where customer demand and a practical ability to deliver overlap.

01

Project purpose

“AI services” is a broad category. Our work is to narrow it into something specific: a customer, a problem worth solving, and a service that a team could deliver consistently.

02

What we’re assessing

The team is comparing potential offerings, target segments, competitive positioning, and go-to-market options. Our discussions have included generative engine optimization (GEO), marketing technology (MarTech), and synthetic data.

03

Current focus

We’re currently putting more attention on GEO and MarTech while keeping synthetic data on the back burner. The next step is to make the offerings concrete enough to evaluate: what the customer receives, what delivery requires, and why the service would be useful.

04

My role

As project team lead, I help coordinate the work and bring the team’s research into a recommendation. The assessment is still in progress. This page shares the project’s direction, not a final client result.
