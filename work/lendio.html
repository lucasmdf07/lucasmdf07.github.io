BYU MBA / Team strategy project

Lendio marketplace strategy

Our team studied Lendio’s small-business lending marketplace, including how borrowers and lenders interact and what follow-up could look like for borrowers who do not receive a suitable offer.

My role
Team strategy project member
When
Fall 2026
Setting
BYU MBA coursework
Focus
Marketplace economics
01

Project purpose

A lending marketplace needs to work for two sides. Small businesses want suitable funding, while lenders evaluate which borrowers fit their requirements. A change that helps one side may affect the other.

02

What we looked at

We analyzed the interaction between borrower needs and lender offers, then considered the borrowers who leave without a suitable match. That led us to examine whether better follow-up and additional information could support a future match.

03

The tradeoff

A follow-up concept needs more than a potential conversion benefit. It also needs to make sense for the marketplace: how lenders use the information, how the borrower experience changes, and whether the process supports the relationship between both sides.

04

My takeaway

The interesting part was learning to evaluate an idea as part of a system. A feature can sound useful on its own, but its value depends on what it changes for everyone involved. This page summarizes student analysis, not a launched Lendio feature.
