Chapter 03

Review Questions

1. What version of HTML will we use in this course?

  • Answer: 5

2. Write the HTML 5 document type declaration as it should appear at the top of all HTML 5 documents. Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <!DOCTYPE HTML>

3. What is the first tag in an HTML 5 document? Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <html>

4. What are the two main sections that every HTML 5 document must have?

  • head
  • body
  • a level 1 paragraph
  • paragraph

5. Inside which section does the <title> tag belong?

  • head
  • body
  • a level 1 paragraph
  • paragraph

6. What is the tag to begin the largest type of heading? Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <h1>

7. What is the tag to begin a paragraph? Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <p>

8. 1. Write the HTML tag and its attributes for creating a text field. Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <input type="text">

9. 1. Write the opening HTML tag and its attributes for creating a button. Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: <button type="button">

10. What is the HTML attribute that a developer can use to uniquely identify each HTML document element?

  • Answer: id

11. What are the characters that begin an HTML comment?

  • Answer: <!--

12. What is the tag that ends an HTML document? Include the surrounding less than (<) and greater than (>) symbols in your answer.

  • Answer: </html>
