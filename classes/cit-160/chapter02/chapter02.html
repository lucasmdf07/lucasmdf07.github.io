Your program will read from the keyboard the number of regular hours and overtime hours that the employee worked and the employee’s regular hourly wage. The employee is paid a bonus of 1.5 times regular pay for each overtime hour worked. Tax is 15% of the employee’s gross pay.

Number each of these steps to solve the problem in the correct order from first (1) to last (7).

  • 6. Subtract tax from gross pay to get net pay. (netPay = grossPay - tax)
